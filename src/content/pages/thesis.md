We offer master thesis and research internship projects at the intersection of bioinformatics, machine learning, and mass spectrometry — suitable for students in computer science, bioinformatics, biomedical sciences, and related fields.
All projects are supervised by lab members and provide hands-on experience with real-world data and open-source software development.

The full list of available projects is maintained on the University of Antwerp <a href="https://esp.uantwerpen.be/projects?employee=Wout+Bittremieux" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Education Support Platform (ESP)</a>.

## How to Apply

Interested? Send an email to [Wout Bittremieux](/contact) with a brief motivation (a few sentences on why the topic interests you) and your transcript of records.
We will schedule a short meeting to discuss your background and find the best match.
Custom project proposals related to our research areas are also welcome.

For students enrolled at the University of Antwerp, projects are registered through the <a href="https://esp.uantwerpen.be/projects?employee=Wout+Bittremieux" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ESP platform</a>.
Guidelines and assessment forms for the master thesis (including deadlines, see Article 39) can be found there as well.
